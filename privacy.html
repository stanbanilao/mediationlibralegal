Privacy notice

Libra Mediation is committed to handling personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable South African privacy law, including the Protection of Personal Information Act where it applies.

Information we may collect

Information may include identity and contact details, account and creditor information, correspondence, financial information, documents you provide and information required to assess or support a mediation matter.

Why information is used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, assess whether mediation is suitable, communicate with authorised parties, administer the service, comply with legal obligations and protect legitimate business interests.

Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers or professionals where necessary for the requested service, subject to appropriate authority and applicable law. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. Information should only be retained for as long as reasonably required for the relevant purpose or legal obligation.

Your rights

You may request access to or correction of personal information held about you, subject to applicable law. Final contact details for privacy requests will be published once confirmed.
