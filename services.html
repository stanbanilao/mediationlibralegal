Services

Practical mediation for difficult debt conversations.

We focus on understanding the facts, identifying what is actually negotiable and helping consumers pursue a documented path forward.

Core services

Support built around the real issue.

Not every matter needs the same intervention. The first step is deciding whether mediation is appropriate at all.

DM

Debt Mediation

Structured engagement where a consumer is struggling to keep up with debt and wants to explore a mutually acceptable path.

CN

Creditor Negotiation

Assistance preparing and communicating repayment proposals, settlement discussions or requests for revised terms.

AR

Arrears Arrangements

Support when home, vehicle, loan or other repayments have fallen behind and the consumer wants to address the arrears.

DB

Disputed Balance Review

Organise statements, notices and payment history where the amount claimed or the account position is disputed.

ADR

Alternative Dispute Resolution

Help frame the issues and possible settlement positions so the parties can engage more constructively.

RF

Professional Referral

Where a matter requires debt-review, regulatory or court-based work, we identify the need for the appropriate registered professional instead of presenting mediation as a substitute.

When mediation may help

Common situations we can assess.

  • You have received a demand, Section 129 notice or collection communication.
  • Repayments on a vehicle, property or credit agreement are in arrears.
  • You want to propose a realistic repayment arrangement.
  • You dispute a balance, fee, interest calculation or payment history.
  • You want to explore settlement before a dispute becomes more costly.
  • You need clarity on whether mediation, debt review or legal advice is the correct route.
What we do not promise

Mediation creates a process — not a guaranteed result.

A creditor is not automatically required to accept a proposal. Mediation does not itself stop litigation, repossession or enforcement, and it does not automatically remove negative credit information. We explain the limits early so you can make an informed decision.
