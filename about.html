About Libra Mediation

Practical resolution starts with a balanced view of the facts.

Libra Mediation is positioned as the mediation and negotiation arm of the wider Libra Legal SA service family.

Our approach

We do not start with a promise. We start with the position.

Consumers often reach out when the situation already feels urgent: arrears are growing, creditors are calling, or legal letters have arrived. Our role is to slow the problem down enough to understand what is actually happening, what can be negotiated and what requires a different professional route.

The aim is simple: clear communication, realistic proposals and properly documented next steps.

01

Clarity first

We separate account facts, affordability, disputes and legal stage before recommending a negotiation path.

02

Balanced communication

Mediation works best when both the consumer's reality and the creditor's position are understood.

03

Correct route

Where the matter is outside mediation, we do not present mediation as a replacement for debt review, regulatory or legal processes.

Service relationships

Libra Mediation can work alongside specialist service providers.

Some cases may require credit information, debt-counselling input, legal representation or other regulated services. Where external providers are involved, their role should be disclosed clearly and they remain responsible for their own regulated services.

We do not state or imply that Libra Mediation is itself a registered credit bureau unless and until that registration exists.
