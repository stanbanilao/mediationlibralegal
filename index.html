What mediation can do

Turn a difficult debt conversation into a structured process.

Debt mediation is an alternative dispute-resolution approach. It does not erase debt or force a creditor to accept a proposal. Its value is in creating a focused process for information, negotiation and possible agreement.

How the process works

One matter. Six clear stages.

We keep the journey understandable so you know what is happening, what is still required and what the next decision is.

01 · ASSESS

Tell us what happened

We review the current debt position, arrears, notices and your objective.

02 · VERIFY

Gather the facts

Relevant statements, notices and supporting documents help define the real issue.

03 · PLAN

Build a realistic proposal

We help structure an offer or engagement path based on affordability and the facts.

04 · ENGAGE

Open the conversation

The appropriate creditor, collector or representative is approached through the agreed channel.

05 · DOCUMENT

Record the position

Any accepted arrangement should be documented clearly so each party understands the terms.

06 · FOLLOW THROUGH

Keep the matter moving

We track next steps and explain when a different regulated or legal route may be required.

Important distinction

Mediation is not the same thing as debt review.

Debt mediation is negotiation-based. It does not automatically place you under debt review, remove a debt-review flag, suspend legal enforcement or compel a creditor to accept reduced payments. Where debt review, court proceedings or regulated legal work is involved, the correct professional route must be used.

This website provides general information only. Every matter depends on its own documents, creditor status and legal stage.
